# Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

"The Bold Lab" is a private tool used only by the owner of The Bold Lab channel to publish that channel's own videos. It is not offered to other users.

## What it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool asks for:

- **Upload videos** (`youtube.upload`): to upload the channel's own videos.
- **View the YouTube account** (`youtube.readonly`): to confirm which channel is connected and read its videos' public statistics.
- **View YouTube Analytics** (`yt-analytics.readonly`): to read views and audience retention for the channel's own videos.

When the owner connects Instagram, the tool uses Meta's Instagram API to publish Reels to the owner's own Instagram account and read that account's Reel insights.

## How the data is used and stored

- It is used only to publish the channel's videos and to decide which videos to make next.
- Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer.
- No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
- The tool never accesses anyone else's account or data.

## Google and YouTube

The Bold Lab's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. The tool uses YouTube API Services; see the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google account at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), and from Instagram under Settings, Apps and websites.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: message The Bold Lab through its YouTube channel or Instagram account.
