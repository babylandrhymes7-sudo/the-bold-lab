# The Bold Lab

Hindi and Hinglish 2D animated explainers about science, the human body and technology: how things really work, in simple words. Posted as YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

The Bold Lab uses a small private tool, run on the channel owner's own computer, to upload its own videos to its own YouTube channel and Instagram account and to read those accounts' view and retention numbers.
